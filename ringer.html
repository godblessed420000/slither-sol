HOW TO PLAY
  • Marbles are set in an X inside a chalk ring. Take turns firing your big shooter (taw) into the pack.
  • Place your shooter anywhere on the ring line, then drag back and release to fire — like a slingshot.
  • Any marble knocked completely outside the ring is yours to keep.
  • Knock one out and you shoot again, from wherever your shooter came to rest — up to the shots-per-turn limit (set it to UNLIMITED for the purist rule).
  • Miss and your shooter stays in the pot — rivals who knock it out of the ring steal a marble from you.
  • Ring empty = game over. Most marbles wins.
