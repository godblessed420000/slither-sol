📖 HOW TO PLAY
  • Marbles sit in an X inside the ring. Fire your big shooter into the pack.
  • Click the ring to place, then drag back & release like a slingshot.
  • Knock a marble fully outside the ring and it's yours.
  • Score and you shoot again, from where your shooter lies.
  • Miss and it stays in the pot — rivals can knock it out and steal a marble.
  • Ring empty = game over. Most marbles wins the pot.
